# Women's Safety Incident Heatmap

karun hav ahe mla karun deya

**Problem:** Women's-safety incidents are reported but rarely analysed by *where* and *when* they happen, so safer routes and patrol planning rely on guesswork.
**Solution:** A web app that maps incidents as a live heatmap, analyses risk by area and time of day, and plans safer journeys. Built with **Flask, SQLite, Leaflet.js and Chart.js**.

> **Demo only.** All data is synthetic (randomly generated for Pune areas) and the demo accounts are public. Do not use real personal data.
